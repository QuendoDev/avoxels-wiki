This is the official wiki of **Avoxels**, for players, modpack makers and addon creators. It is available in English and Spanish; when a page has not been translated yet, the English version is shown.

## How pages are made

- **Reference pages** are generated automatically from the mod's own data every time it changes, so they always match the current version.
- **Guides and explanations** are written by hand.
- Generated pages can also carry hand-written notes. Those notes live apart from the generated text, so updates never overwrite them.
